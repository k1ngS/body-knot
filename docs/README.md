## Honest scope

The Mind did not directly invent or implement BODY//KNOT's final self-crossing knot mechanic, Focus cursor, eye reveal, or Host Core finale.

Its meaningful contributions were the infected 32×32 host/immune-system framing, parasite pressure, reactive cable ancestry, constraint-driven design, short-form judge pacing, minimalist readability, accessibility, and the concept of the system becoming alive.

The final mechanical synthesis and implementation were developed under human direction with ChatGPT and Codex.
