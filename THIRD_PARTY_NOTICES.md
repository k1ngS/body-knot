# Third-Party Notices

## Gemini Text-to-Speech

The final spoken voice assets used in BODY//KNOT were generated as
static audio files with Gemini Text-to-Speech.

Voice direction, selection, editing, cutting, loudness adjustment and
integration were performed by Marcos Beltrão.

No Gemini API, authentication key or online generation service is
included in the released game.

## Audacity

Audacity was used to edit, cut, normalize and export the final voice
recordings.

Audacity is not bundled with the game.

## Minds by Animoca Brands

The Game Designer Mind was used as a design collaborator during the
project's development and prototype-evaluation process.

## Procedural Audio Pack

The files located under:

- `public/audio/ambience/`
- `public/audio/sfx/`

were created specifically for BODY//KNOT through procedural synthesis.

No third-party sound samples were included in those generated files.
